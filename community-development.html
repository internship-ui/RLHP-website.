Life Skills

Adolescent & Youth Empowerment

Targeted skill enhancement in slums and rural areas, mentoring youth in personal growth, HRD, gender sensitization, and eco-protection.

High-impact adolescent empowerment in Gundlupete with APPI.
Market-driven vocational and livelihood skill development.
Youth action groups driving environmental conservation.
Rights & Schemes

People's Development & Last-Mile Schemes

Bridging the gap between the government and marginalized families in backward regions like Bidar, Gulbarga, Raichur, Chitradurga, and Davanagere.

Covering 21 Gram Panchayats across 100 backward villages.
Capacitating VHSNCs, SDMCs & MSDF for civic monitoring.
Assisting slum dwellers in securing official land title deeds.
Relief & Restoration

Humanitarian Assistance & Disaster Relief

Rapid response and long-term rehabilitation during natural calamities and pandemics, ensuring shelter, livelihoods, and psychosocial restoration.

5-Year post-Tsunami rehabilitation in Tamil Nadu (2005-2010).
Kerala Flood relief supporting 6,000+ families in Wayanad & Alappuzha.
COVID-19 relief: 5,000+ ration kits & continuous digital IEP learning.
Eco Action

Natural Resource Management

Environmental protection, organic farming promotion, and community-driven water conservation in rural Karnataka to combat climate distress.

Organic farming techniques & seed distribution for smallholders.
Lake restoration & water table replenishment in Mysuru villages.
School eco-clubs & extensive tree plantation campaigns.
Collective Voice

Networking & State-Level Advocacy

Lead role in state and national coalitions for effective policy advocacy, child rights defense, and social justice legislation.

State & National Secretariat for Campaign Against Child Labour (CACL).
Active partner in CACT, KCRO, PAPRE & Right to Food/Health.
Steering district networks MAITHRI, FEVORD-K, MSDF & DWANI.
