Who We Are

Rooted in Grassroots Participation for Over 4 Decades

Registered on 24th May 1984 under the Karnataka Societies Registration Act 1960 (No. 17 of 1960, 23/84-85), RLHP operates with the conviction that true transformation happens when communities own their development.

Headquartered in Mysuru, RLHP coordinates programme activities across Mysuru, Bidar, Kalaburagi, and Chamarajanagar project offices. We combine field action with state-level advocacy, working closely with district administrations, panchayats, and like-minded alliances.

Our Vision

To create a just, free, equitable and sustainable society where every human being lives with dignity and rights.

Our Mission

To empower children, women, and deprived sections in urban, rural, and disaster-affected areas to respond to poverty and rights violations through collective affirmative action.

Community Development Case Studies

Construction of a CC Road in Kashiram Thanda

For more than 30 years, over 50 households in the remote tribal hamlet of Kashiram Thanda lacked a motorable road, facing extreme isolation during monsoons. Through RLHP's Mass Contact Programme and civic training, the villagers petitioned the Gram Panchayat and Taluk administration, successfully securing a ₹15 Lakh cement concrete road sanction that transformed village access.

Infrastructure Advocacy Kashiram Thanda, North Karnataka 50+ Families Connected

Community Action Against Illegal Liquor Sales

In Mirzapur village (Basavakalyana Taluk), widespread unlicensed liquor sales severely eroded family livelihoods and endangered women and children. Empowered by RLHP leadership workshops, local women sangha federations led peaceful pickets, filed formal representations with the Excise Department and Police Station, shutting down illegal dens and restoring village peace.

Women Collective Action Mirzapur, Basavakalyana Illicit Outlets Closed

Breaking Gender Barriers Through Folk Art

In Bannithalapura village, traditional Kamsale folk dance had been strictly reserved for men for generations. Adolescent girls from the RLHP-formed Kiran Bedi Kishori Group broke this deep-seated gender taboo by formally training in Kamsale rhythm and dance, performing with poise at district cultural forums and earning wide community respect.

Cultural Empowerment Bannithalapura, Gundlupete State Recognition
